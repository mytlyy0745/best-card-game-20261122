**Ember Duel** — a turn-based card battler against the Cinder Warden. Both sides start at 25 health; draw a card, gain mana, play ember-spirit creatures and spark spells, and burn the Warden to 0 before your own flame goes out. Creatures strike at the start of your turn, damage spells can snipe the Warden or their creatures, and three difficulties (Apprentice, Adept, Master) change the Warden's health, hand, and how often it blunders.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests — the game is plain static files under `game/`.

Mouse: click a card to play it (damage spells ask you to pick a target). Keys: `1–7` play a card, `E` ends your turn, `R` restarts after a win or loss.
